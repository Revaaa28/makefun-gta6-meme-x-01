**Calling in sick for GTA VI launch day.** An original meme made for GTA VI's launch day, 19 November 2026: make.fun's ghost mascot — sparkle eyes, magenta star coin and all — sits on his couch in a shirt and tie, phone to his ear, fake-coughing his way through a sick call while a controller waits in his other hand and Vice City glows on the TV. In the doorway behind him stands his boss, also holding a controller, caught. Top caption: "CALLING IN SICK FOR GTA VI LAUNCH DAY". Bottom caption: "MY BOSS: 'SEE YOU IN LEONIDA'".

The meme was posted on X by [@lmyuunn34](https://x.com/lmyuunn34/status/2123432696575664613) on launch day; its text tags @makefunai, names make.fun and carries the play code, and the post itself is the entry. This repository hands the post in: `POST.json` names it, and `main.ts` copies `POST.json` to the output directory where the gate reads it. Nothing the program writes is judged — the house reads the post from X and an AI judge compares the posts two at a time.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
